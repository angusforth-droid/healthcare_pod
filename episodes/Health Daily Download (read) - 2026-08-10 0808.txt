## SOUTH KOREA

**Cardiovascular and Metabolic Safety Data**
- GLP-1 receptor agonists semaglutide and liraglutide associated with increased psychiatric, gastrointestinal, and hepatic adverse events in 13 South Korean hospitals (2018-2025); semaglutide cohort 2,357 initiators showed depression HR 3.42, anxiety HR 2.39, gastrointestinal dysmotility HR 3.91; liraglutide cohort 6,953 initiators showed depression HR 1.51, hepatic impairment HR 1.46, pancreatitis/cholangitis/cholelithiasis HR 1.40. Diabetes, Obesity and Metabolism.

**Cardiovascular Outcomes**
- Multicentre retrospective cohort study of 5,405 acute coronary syndrome patients undergoing percutaneous coronary intervention across five Korean hospitals found laboratory-based frailty index (FI-Lab ≥0.4) associated with 1.95x increased hospital readmission hazard ratio (95% CI 1.635–2.316) and 3.68x increased all-cause mortality (95% CI 2.680–5.038); 25.2% of cohort classified as frail. European Journal of Cardiovascular Nursing.

## CHINA

**Digital Health and Rehabilitation**
- Clinical trial results from Cangnan County Hospital of Traditional Chinese Medicine in British Journal of Hospital Medicine: 147 post-operative lumbar disc herniation patients (72 intervention, 75 control) receiving three-month empowerment-based rehabilitation with WeChat digital support showed lower pain (VAS 3.00 vs 4.00, p<0.001), better function (ODI 28.00 vs 32.00, p=0.005), higher self-efficacy (p<0.001), improved quality of life (p<0.001), and higher nursing satisfaction (93.06% vs 78.67%, p=0.013). British Journal of Hospital Medicine.
