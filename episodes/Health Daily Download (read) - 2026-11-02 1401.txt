**Conversational Health AI**
- Fullspan Health's conversational AI agent Healthline AI launched in June and accumulated 1.1 million views and 13,500 daily users by August 18; 75% of users submit queries, 94% of actions involve care pathway navigation or provider finding, 78% positive feedback, zero reported clinical or compliance incidents across 2,800+ diagnostic interactions (Fierce Healthcare)

**Quality Measures Development**
- Komodo Health and National Committee for Quality Assurance announced multiyear collaboration to develop and validate healthcare quality measures leveraging real-world data analytics (MobiHealthNews)

**Cybersecurity Operations**
- U.S. Department of Justice, Federal Bureau of Investigation, and National Security Agency seized intrusion platforms operated by QTFY, a People's Republic of China-sponsored cyber threat group targeting U.S. critical infrastructure (Healthcare IT News)

**Life Expectancy Improvement**
- CDC National Center for Health Statistics released 2024 life expectancy data showing US life expectancy reached 79 years at birth, up 0.6 years from 2023 and surpassing pre-pandemic 2019 levels by 0.2 years, driven by declining COVID deaths, unintentional injuries, heart disease, cancer, and homicide deaths (STAT News)

- Pennsylvania Department of Health reports two measles-related deaths in Lancaster County, Pennsylvania—first measles deaths in the U.S. in 2025; both deceased individuals were unvaccinated (STAT News)

**GLP-1 Medicare Coverage**

**Cardiovascular Drug Approval**
- Eli Lilly's Mounjaro (tirzepatide) received FDA approval for reducing major adverse cardiovascular events in patients with heart disease (Endpoints News)

**GLP-1 Compounding Restrictions**
- U.S. Appeals Court upheld FDA determinations ending shortage declarations for Novo Nordisk's Ozempic/Wegovy and Eli Lilly's Mounjaro/Zepbound; rejected Outsourcing Facilities Association legal challenge restricting compounders' mass production of GLP-1 biosimilars (STAT News)

**Alzheimer's Blood Biomarker**
- FDA cleared Elecsys pTau217 blood test developed by Roche and Eli Lilly for Alzheimer's disease assessment in patients age 55+ with cognitive decline; Labcorp and Quest Diagnostics announced commercial availability; Quest plans nationwide rollout Q4 2026 with expanded AD-Detect panels in 2027 (MedTech Dive)

**Autoimmune Hemolytic Anemia Treatment**
- FDA approved Johnson & Johnson's Imaavy for warm autoimmune hemolytic anemia in patients aged 12+ currently or previously treated with steroids; approval based on study of 115 adults; disease prevalence 1 in 8,000 people (STAT News Pharmalot)

**Dermatomyositis Treatment**
- FDA approves Lisraya (brepocitinib) developed by Roivant Sciences subsidiary Priovant Therapeutics for dermatomyositis (STAT News)

**Polycythemia Vera Treatment**
- FDA approves rusfertide (Mimrylo), developed by Takeda Pharmaceutical and Protagonist Therapeutics, for polycythemia vera; first-in-class hepcidin mimetic (Endpoints News)

**Gastric Cancer Label Expansion**
- Jazz Pharmaceuticals receives FDA approval for Ziihera (glofitamab) label expansion in frontline gastric cancer, expanding from treatment-resistant to newly diagnosed patient populations (Endpoints News)

**Pancreatic Cancer Treatment**
- Revolution Medicines receives FDA approval for Rasonque, a pancreatic cancer treatment showing approximately 100% improvement in patient survival metrics (Endpoints Weekly)

**PTSD Digital Intervention Study**
- Uniformed Services University launched clinical study led by Col. (Ret.) Dr. Michael J. Roy to evaluate Mentaily's AI avatar against the Clinician-Administered PTSD Scale for post-traumatic stress disorder diagnosis (MobiHealthNews)

**Wearable Hypertension Screening**
- Researchers developed machine learning framework for hypertension screening using photoplethysmography data from wearables, achieving 84.6% accuracy on external validation (European Heart Journal: Digital Health)

**Diabetes Prediction from ECG**
- Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center developed and validated AIRE-DM, a convolutional neural network using electrocardiography to predict incident type 2 diabetes; external validation in UK Biobank (65,606 participants) and ELSA-Brasil (13,739 participants) showed C-index 0.625-0.688 (European Heart Journal: Digital Health)

**Stigmatizing Language Detection**
- Researchers developed and validated LLM-enhanced natural language processing model detecting stigmatizing language in electronic health records of substance use disorder patients; unprofessional language appeared in 72% of SUD notes vs 52% of non-SUD notes and was associated with 1.54x higher odds of behavioral health referral (Digital Health)
