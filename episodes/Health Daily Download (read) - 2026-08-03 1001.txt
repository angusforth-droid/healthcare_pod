## UNITED KINGDOM

**Maternity and Neonatal Care Reform**
- England's Health Secretary appointed the country's first Maternity and Neonatal Care Commissioner with enforcement powers following the Amos Review, which identified systemic failures, racism, and discrimination across NHS maternity services (The Guardian)

**Pharmaceutical Pricing and Access**
- UK pharmaceutical pricing reform increased the National Institute for Health and Care Excellence cost-effectiveness threshold for the first time in 25 years, enabling faster NHS patient access to new medicines across cancer, blood disorders, and autoimmune conditions (The Guardian)
- Novo Nordisk's oral semaglutide tablet now available for private purchase through UK pharmacies, expanding GLP-1 weight-loss medication access beyond injectable Wegovy (BBC News)

**NHS Operational Mandates**
- NHS England mandated minimum 3-week advance notice for all new planned medical appointments, effective immediately (NHS England announcement)
- NHS England announced mandatory publication of league tables ranking acute, ambulance, and mental health trusts on staff wellbeing metrics including racism, violence, and sexual misconduct, effective July 2025, covering 1.5+ million NHS staff (MedCity News)

**Consultant Industrial Action**
- Consultant doctors in England voted to authorize strikes over 12-month period demanding higher pay and shorter working week, citing 25% real-terms salary decline since 2008-09 (The Guardian)
- Consultants in England secured 12-month strike mandate through British Medical Association ballot vote over pay and working conditions (BBC News)

**Regional Innovation Investment**
- Greater Manchester secured £11 million funding to establish wearables and remote monitoring innovation cluster (HTN Health Tech News)

**Healthcare System Technology**
- Lincolnshire Partnership NHS Foundation Trust procured interface engine for healthcare system data integration (HTN Health Tech News)
- Partnership agreement established healthtech innovation hub at Old Medical School in Leeds city centre involving Leeds Teaching Hospitals NHS Trust, University of Leeds, and Leeds City Council (Building Better Healthcare)

**Clinical Trial Implementation**
- TRAK MSK randomised feasibility trial completed across NHS physiotherapy sites: 75 participants randomised, 60% retention at 16 weeks, positive directional outcomes in pain and physical activity (BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders)

**Dementia Research Infrastructure**
- UK Government's Scientific Advisory Board released recommendations under Dame Barbara Windsor Dementia Goals Programme to establish Brain Aging Registry for Biomarkers, Access to trials, Research and Adoption and implement biomarker-driven early diagnosis pathways (UK Government)

## UNITED STATES

**Regulatory and Policy**

- FDA appointed eight panelists to committee advising on peptide compounding with majority having commercial interests in peptide promotion (STAT News)
- FDA digital health leader signaled updated artificial intelligence policy guidance forthcoming (FDA)
- FDA set decision deadline for end of February 2025 on Sarepta Therapeutics' exon-skipping drug regulatory pathway (Endpoints News)
- Karim Mikhail assumed acting directorship of FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research following Vijay Kumar's departure (Healthcare Dive)
- Eli Lilly, Regeneron, Cellares, Fujifilm, Kriya Therapeutics, Amneal Pharmaceuticals, and one unnamed company selected for FDA's PreCheck pilot program providing early collaborative feedback on drug development and manufacturing strategies (Endpoints News)

**Medicare and Insurance Policy**

- Trump administration negotiated $245 monthly price cap for GLP-1 drugs with Eli Lilly and Novo Nordisk in November 2024, conditioned on private Medicare insurers covering GLP-1s for all uses with $50 copay (STAT News)
- ACA marketplace enrollment declined by nearly 3 million following expiration of enhanced federal subsidies in 2024 (Healthcare Dive)
- Department of Education implemented RISE student loan rules capping graduate student borrowing at $20,500 annually while preserving $50,000 threshold for 11 designated professional degrees, excluding nurse practitioners and physician assistants; American Nurses Association challenged policy (STAT News)
- Twenty-six U.S. states filed legal challenge against Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services work requirements rule; requesting preliminary injunction to halt implementation pending litigation (Healthcare Dive)
- Twenty-six-state coalition sued Trump administration over Medicaid work requirements rollout (FierceHealthcare)
- CMS proposed 2027 Outpatient Prospective Payment System rule with 2.4% base rate increase offset by steep cuts to 340B drug program payments and expansion of site-neutral payment policies to off-campus imaging services (MedCity News)
- Kaiser Family Foundation estimated Medicare will allocate $13.4 billion to Medicare Advantage Quality Bonus Program in 2026 (MedCity News)

**Medical Device Approvals and Acquisitions**

- Edwards Lifesciences won FDA clearance for LARIAT left atrial appendage clip, competing with AtriCure and Medtronic in LAA closure market (MedTech Dive)
