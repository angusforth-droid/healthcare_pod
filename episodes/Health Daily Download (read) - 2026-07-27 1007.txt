## UNITED STATES

**DIGITAL HEALTH & AI**

- Prosper AI secured $30 million Series A funding led by Andreessen Horowitz with Base10, Emergence Capital, Y Combinator, and Company Ventures for healthcare voice AI agents. MobiHealthNews.

- Cadence closed $100 million Series C funding led by Spark Capital with co-investment from Duke Health, Memorial Hermann, and Corewell Health Ventures for chronic care management platform. MobiHealthNews.

- Subtle Medical secured $33 million Series C funding and appointed new CEO; AI-powered medical imaging startup expanding deployment across hospital networks and imaging centers. Fierce Healthcare.

- Midtown AI raised $30 million Series A led by Andreessen Horowitz for healthcare billing automation. Crain's New York Business.

- Epic Systems rolling out enterprise resource planning system integrating financial management, supply chain, human resources, and revenue cycle management alongside existing EHR capabilities. Axios.

- Reid Health deployed Abridge AI documentation technology for nursing staff; after-shift charting reduced by up to 45 minutes per shift, RN vacancy rate cut by more than 50 percent. MedCity News.

- Innovaccer and Amazon Web Services announced strategic partnership integrating Innovaccer's healthcare data platform with AWS cloud infrastructure; Mount Sinai Health System adopted Signal 1 AI-powered clinical management platform for operational decision-making. Fierce Healthcare.

- TriNetX LIVE Network surpassed 300 million patient records, expanding real-world evidence capabilities globally. TriNetX.

- FDA closed enforcement action against Whoop after it removed clinical-grade blood pressure claims and repositioned feature as relative trend indicator. STAT News.

**HEALTH SYSTEMS & OPERATIONS**

- Thoreau Group acquired Ensemble Health Partners for approximately $12 billion in healthcare revenue cycle management and clinical documentation consolidation. Bloomberg.

- Kaiser Permanente Colorado and Nymbl published retrospective cohort study (3,735 Nymbl users versus 114,219 non-users, aged 60 and older, February 2018 to September 2024) showing self-guided balance exercise app associated with 3.83 percentage point reduction in acute fall-related injuries in older adults with prior fall history. Journal of the American Geriatrics Society.

- LucyRx and Abarca Health announced merger to build scale amid pharmacy benefit management industry consolidation. Fierce Healthcare.

- Lown Institute research findings reveal top-ranked hospitals underperforming on health equity initiatives and accountability measures. Lown Institute.

**FRAUD & ENFORCEMENT**

- U.S. Department of Justice announced criminal charges against 455 defendants for participation in healthcare fraud schemes including fraudulent amniotic wound allograft sales and Medicaid billing for services never delivered. FierceHealthcare.

**POLICY & RESEARCH**

- Kevin Schulman, Stanford University School of Medicine Clinical Excellence Research Center, published opinion in STAT News proposing FDA policy shift to encourage independent laboratory testing of generic drugs; generic drugs represent 90 percent of U.S. prescriptions. STAT News.

- West Health-Gallup Affordability Index survey finds 49 percent of U.S. adults classified as "Cost Secure," down 2 percentage points from 2024 and 12 points from 2022. FierceHealthcare.

- Columbia University Medical Center and NPR published peer-reviewed study on movement break frequency recruiting 20,000 participants across all 50 U.S. states over three years in British Journal of Sports Medicine. STAT News.

**LIFE SCIENCES & THERAPEUTICS**

- Eli Lilly and Abbisko Therapeutics expanded drug discovery collaboration with up to $1.9 billion in potential milestone payments. Endpoints News.

- Osanni Bio raised $190 million Series B to establish ophthalmology and cardiology affiliate companies. Endpoints News.

- Abiologics, backed by Flagship Pioneering, demonstrated AI-driven design of mirror-image proteins using D-amino acids for novel therapeutic proteins with enhanced durability. Endpoints News.

- LivaNova's VITARIA vagus nerve stimulator failed to meet efficacy endpoints in FDA breakthrough-designated pivotal trial for heart failure; independent monitoring committee determined in 2022 that trial signals insufficient to warrant early stopping after enrolling 500 of 1,000 planned patients. - Oura and LillyDirect announced partnership providing free Oura Ring sizing kits to customers using GLP-1 therapies. MobiHealthNews.

**DIGITAL HEALTH & PLATFORMS**

- Ladder Health raised $7 million seed round led by Nina Capital for virtual-first pediatric therapy model. MedCity News.

- Dassault Systèmes launching virtual twin and sovereign cloud platform for European health-tech startups. DigiTimes.

**PUBLIC HEALTH & POLICY**

- WHO released guidance calling for scale-up of newborn screening programmes; 8 million babies born annually with birth defects, 90 percent of serious cases in low- and middle-income countries; case studies from Argentina, Brazil, Egypt, India showing India's three-year programme identified 900,000 children with defects. WHO.

- Julie Richard, France Ministry of Health, identified data normalization between EU member states as critical need for cross-border health data sharing under European Health Data Space framework. France Ministry of Health.

**RESEARCH & EVIDENCE**

- World Happiness Report research finds increased social media usage correlates with reduced individual wellbeing scores. BBC News.

- Medtronic awarded first 100 Medtronic Spark Scholarships to students pursuing health technology careers in inaugural year of program. Medtronic.

- Systematic scoping review of 681 randomised controlled trials registered 2018-2025 across WHO International Clinical Trials Registry Platform, ClinicalTrials.gov, European Union Clinical Trials Register examining digital psychotherapies for adults with depression; 51.8 percent completed, 34.1 percent published results, 78.1 percent prospectively registered, 8.5 percent industry-funded, 61.8 percent excluded patients with suicidal ideation. BMJ Mental Health.
