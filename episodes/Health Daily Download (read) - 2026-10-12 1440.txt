## GLOBAL (MULTI-COUNTRY & REGIONAL)

**Life Sciences: Oncology & Immunology**

- Roche/Genentech's obinutuzumab β met primary endpoint in BeInmost phase 3 trial for AQP4-IgG-seropositive neuromyelitis optica spectrum disorder, with 4.4% relapse rate versus 45.7% placebo (hazard ratio 0.069, P<0.0001) and 93.1% relative risk reduction over 52 weeks. Nature Medicine.

- Novartis tisagenlecleucel (CD19 CAR T) demonstrated decade-long persistence in 38 adult patients with relapsed/refractory B-cell non-Hodgkin lymphoma, with CAR T cells detectable up to 10.1 years post-infusion in five of eight long-term responders and 10-year lymphoma-free survival of 32% (large B-cell lymphoma) and 47% (follicular lymphoma). Nature.

- Meta-analysis of 42 studies (2018-2024) demonstrates FDA/CE-cleared AI systems for mammography screening achieve pooled AUC 0.89 (95% CI: 0.85-0.92), sensitivity 76.3%, specificity 89.6%; prospective workflow studies show non-inferior cancer detection with significant radiologist workload reduction. European Journal of Radiology.

**Digital Health & AI**

- Digital health interventions reduce heart failure hospitalizations by 27% and all-cause mortality by 14% in low- and middle-income countries; systematic review of 15 RCTs (n=4,568) across Brazil, China, Thailand published in European Heart Journal: Digital Health, August 2026.

- CINEX (Clinical Information Extraction Reporting Guideline) published in Journal of the American Medical Informatics Association; 29-item consensus checklist for standardizing natural language processing and large language model studies in clinical settings, developed through 3-round Delphi process with 20 international experts.

- Robotic total knee arthroplasty achieves 1.1-1.3mm mean absolute error in ligament gap assessment across 672 consecutive procedures and five surgeons. Journal of Robotic Surgery, August 2026.

- OpenAI o4-mini and Google Gemini 2.5 Flash models classify COVID-19 vaccination stance from social media with 81.9% and 79.9% accuracy respectively, but show systematic reasoning failures in 14.9% of dual-misclassified tweets. Journal of Medical Systems, August 2026.

- JARDIN project establishes consensus-based rare disease care pathway framework across 16 European countries through stakeholder workshops (216 participants) and Delphi survey (88 respondents), achieving 100% first-round consensus across seven domains. European Journal of Medical Genetics.

- European Society of Endocrine Surgeons published consensus statements establishing standardized protocols for intraoperative nerve monitoring documentation, interpretation, and reporting in endocrine surgery, addressing substantial heterogeneity in current practice across European centers. British Journal of Surgery, August 2026.

- Global access to different classes of diabetes medicines: a framework for targeted policy actions published by Emory University, Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health, University of Cape Town, Indian Council of Medical Research, Heidelberg University. Nature Medicine. 530 million individuals globally have type 2 diabetes; only 1 in 5 achieve glycemic control; economic losses estimated at $10.2 trillion (2020-2050).

**Digital Health & AI: Wearables & Monitoring**

- Sighing dynamics identified as digital biomarker for anxiety in wearable respiratory monitoring study. Hexoskin smart shirt, 38 participants (15 with anxiety disorders, 23 healthy controls), 1,279 psychophysiological windows, 79.5% EMA completion rate. JMIR Formative Research. August 2026.

- UK Biobank study published in Journal of Neural Transmission (August 2026) demonstrating wrist-worn accelerometer sensors detected gait abnormalities predictive of Parkinson's disease diagnosis up to 6.8 years before clinical diagnosis in 73,413 participants with 314 developing PD, using Watch Walk algorithm identifying five consistent gait biomarkers.

- Study published in Journal of Vestibular Research (August 2026) assessing sociodemographic bias in 12 instruction-tuned large language models across 2,040,000 responses for emergency department dizziness cases finding Black transgender women received 12.2 percentage points lower mental health referral likelihood and Black patients experiencing homelessness received 9.1 percentage points lower referral likelihood.

- Study published in JHEP Reports (August 2026) of 119 cirrhosis patients using Oura Ring wearable technology analyzing 12,516 sleep episodes over 6 months finding Sleep Regularity Index was strongest mediator (49.9% mediation effect) linking liver disease severity to quality of life impairment.

- Wearable device safety study in eating disorder populations; 30 adults with binge-spectrum eating disorders wearing consumer-grade activity trackers for 28 days showed decreased ED symptoms, not increased; maladaptive exercise episodes reduced by 26.43 episodes (p<0.001); loss-of-control eating episodes decreased (OR=0.96, p=0.014); dietary restraint episodes declined by 7.97 episodes (p=0.001). Digital Health, August 2026.
