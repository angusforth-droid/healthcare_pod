# HEALTHCARE AND LIFE SCIENCES BRIEFING

## UNITED STATES

### DIGITAL HEALTH & AI

- Mount Sinai Health System deployed Clearstep AI-driven self-triage integrated with Epic's MyMountSinai app, completing 60,000+ visits with 88-96% concordance with physician triage and zero safety incidents (NEJM Catalyst Innovations in Care Delivery)

- FDA shifted regulatory stance to actively encourage continuous real-time data sharing in clinical trials, moving from theoretical concept to practical expectation in neurology drug development (MedCity News)

- Medicare proposed banning remote monitoring vendors from providing services, requiring healthcare providers to manage RPM programs directly—a significant policy shift affecting the remote patient monitoring market (STAT News)

- Eli Lilly partnered with Oura to integrate wearable ring technology with GLP-1 receptor agonist therapy monitoring for patients on obesity and diabetes treatments (Endpoints News)

### LIFE SCIENCES

- Eli Lilly's Zepbound (tirzepatide) study identified potential frailty signals in older adults, prompting enhanced monitoring recommendations as Medicare expands obesity therapy access (STAT News/Reuters)

- Biogen's diranersen showed Phase 2 trial results with cognitive decline reduction comparable to approved Alzheimer's treatments, supporting progression to Phase 3 (STAT News)

- Nava Therapeutics emerged with 89 million dollar Series B funding for next-generation lipid nanoparticles targeting bone marrow, immune cells, and kidneys (Endpoints News)

### VENTURE & CORPORATE

- Neko Health secured 700 million dollar Series C funding to enter U.S. market with planned New York City clinic opening (Fierce Healthcare)

- Corner Health secured 32.5 million dollar in seed and Series A funding (Health Tech World)
