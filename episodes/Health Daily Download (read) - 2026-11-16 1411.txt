### Life Sciences

- **AstraZeneca** received FDA accelerated approval for camizestrant, an oral selective estrogen receptor degrader (SERD), for breast cancer despite negative advisory committee vote. Endpoints News.

- **AstraZeneca** secured FDA accelerated clearance for Etcamah (breast cancer pill) in first-line HR-positive, HER2-negative breast cancer conditional on ESR1 mutation detection via FDA-authorized companion diagnostic. BiopharmaDive.

- **Novo Nordisk** halted two Phase III cardiovascular trials of ziltivekimab (HERMES and ATHENA) after independent data monitoring committee determined unlikely to meet primary endpoints; previous ZEUS trial showed drug lowered inflammatory markers but failed to reduce major adverse cardiovascular events. STAT News.

- **Roivant Sciences** initiated Phase 3 studies for mosliciguat in pulmonary hypertension after Phase 2 success showing 56% reduction in blood flow resistance through lungs in PH-ILD patients; Leerink Partners projects peak sales exceeding $10 billion annually. BiopharmaDive.

- **Bristol Myers Squibb** reported Phase 2b success for arlocabtagene autoleucel (arlo-cel), CAR-T therapy targeting GPRC5D in multiple myeloma patients who failed ≥4 prior treatment lines including BCMA-directed drugs. BiopharmaDive.

- **Pharvaris** announced Phase 3 trial results for deucrictibant, oral preventive therapy for hereditary angioedema, reducing swelling attack rates by 83% across all three HAE forms; regulatory submission planned for H1 2027. BiopharmaDive.

- **Inhibrx Biosciences** and **Merck** demonstrated INBRX-106 plus Keytruda achieved 48% response rate versus 27% for Keytruda alone in recurrent head and neck squamous cell carcinoma; 72% versus 43% six-month progression-free survival respectively; Inhibrx expanding trials to 50 additional HPV-positive patients. BiopharmaDive.

- **Arrowhead Research (Vistra)** completed Phase 1/2a clinical trial of ARO-RAGE, inhaled siRNA therapeutic targeting RAGE mRNA in lung epithelial cells; 58 healthy volunteers and 19 asthma patients demonstrated safety, tolerability, and dose-responsive reductions in serum soluble RAGE levels with >90% lung RAGE mRNA silencing maintained >8 weeks in preclinical studies. Nature Medicine.

- **Labcorp** acquired **MLM Medical Labs** to expand central laboratory and biomarker capabilities for clinical trials; Labcorp spent $528.6 million on acquisitions in H1 2026 and committed additional $155 million for outreach laboratory assets; central lab service sales grew 9.8% year-over-year in Q2 2026. MedTech Dive.

### Public Health Research

- **University of Oxford** published research in International Journal of Cancer establishing causal link between consuming very hot beverages (≥65°C) and increased oesophageal cancer risk; study analysed 977,282 middle-aged adults; people drinking very hot beverages had approximately 3x higher oesophageal cancer risk versus warm drinkers; researchers estimate 1-2 in 10 oesophageal cancers could be prevented by avoiding very hot drinks. BBC News.

- **University of Oxford** study published in International Journal of Cancer found very hot beverage consumption (70°C or above) triples oesophageal squamous cell carcinoma risk; six or more daily very hot drinks versus fewer than six increases risk 71%; study of 979,000 participants over 14 years; 1 in 7 UK SCC cases could be prevented by switching from very hot to hot temperature; funded by Cancer Research UK. The Guardian.
