# HEALTHCARE & LIFE SCIENCES BRIEFING
## Week of July 2026

---

## UNITED KINGDOM

**Respiratory AI Platform Expansion**
TidalSense raised $19 million Series B funding from Cross-Border Impact Ventures, BGF, Airstream Capital, and Foresight Group to expand its N-Tidal Diagnose platform across Europe and prepare US market entry. The platform received Class IIa CE mark in March 2025 and has been deployed across NHS England, Wales, and Scotland since September 2025, with diagnostic models trained on 2.5 million patient breaths achieving over 90% accuracy. Mobihealthnews.

**Emergency Contraception Access Expansion**
NHS England expanded emergency contraceptive pills through high street pharmacies at no cost, supplying 304,800 doses between October 2025 and March 2026, while oral contraceptive consultations doubled from 50,000 to 115,000 over a 12-month period to February 2026. Fierce Healthcare.

**Lung Transplant Preservation Technology**
NICE recommended XVIVO perfusion system implementation across NHS transplant centres for four-year assessment. The machine preserves donated lungs at body temperature using plasma-mimicking fluid and could rescue approximately 4 in 5 unused lungs, addressing the current 77% discard rate. Newcastle upon Tyne Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust and British Transplantation Society are leading implementation for 170 patients including 6 children waiting for lung transplants. Fierce Healthcare.

**Long COVID Treatment Trial Completion**
STIMULATE-ICP phase 3 trial completed across 122 primary care networks in England with 1,152 participants through NHS England and Coverscan. All intervention arms showed fatigue improvement of 4.5 points from baseline 35.8, though multi-organ MRI and digital rehabilitation showed no significant additional benefit versus usual care. Nature Medicine.

**Care Service Review Acceleration**
Deputy Prime Minister Andy Burnham announced acceleration of Louise Casey's National Care Service review from 2028 to within 12 months, committing to backing Casey's recommendations 100 percent. The Guardian.

**Life Expectancy Regional Disparities**
King's Fund released provisional life expectancy data for England showing widening north-south divide in 2023-25, with regional disparities reaching 3 years between London and north-east, and 10 years between Blackpool and Hart, Hampshire. Overall life expectancy stands at 80 years for men and 84 years for women. The Guardian.

**Cybersecurity Breach**
Craneware plc, a UK healthtech firm, suffered a cyber attack stealing customer and staff data. The company is investigating the breach scope. Craneware.

**Regulatory Innovation Platform**
Psyomics secured an Innovate UK loan to expand its regulated platform across NHS trusts. Health Tech World.

**Pharmaceutical Strategy Announcement**
GlaxoSmithKline announced a three-year strategic plan focused on cost reduction and R&D acceleration under CEO Luke Miels, taking a £1.33 billion charge related to a cough trial failure. Endpoints News.

## UNITED STATES

**AI Respiratory Diagnostics Deployment**
Ardent Health Partners surpassed 1 million patient visits using ambient AI technology for automated clinical documentation. Fierce Healthcare.

**Continuous Glucose Monitoring Monitoring Program**
FDA named Dexcom as the first participant in the TEMPO program for post-market real-world performance monitoring of continuous glucose monitoring devices. Fierce Healthcare.

**Measles Outbreak Escalation**
The US surpassed 2025 measles record with 2,318 confirmed cases as of July 2026, exceeding 2,289 cases in 2025 and marking the worst year since 1991. Vaccination coverage has fallen below the 95 percent threshold, with large outbreaks in South Carolina, Utah, and Arizona. The Guardian.

**Cyclospora Outbreak Response Criticism**
Frank Yiannas, former FDA Deputy Commissioner, accused the Trump administration of catastrophic mismanagement of cyclospora outbreak response, with 4,000+ confirmed cases across nine US states and 7,400 potential cases under investigation. Politico.

**Behavioral Health Outcomes**

CDC National Center for Health Statistics released 2022-2023 survey data showing 6.2 percent endometriosis diagnosis, 8.7 percent PCOS diagnosis, and 7.8 percent uterine fibroids diagnosis among US women ages 20-49, with PCOS diagnosis lowest in Black women at 4.4 percent. CDC.

**Substance Use and Mental Health Survey**
SAMHSA released 2025 National Survey on Drug Use and Health showing 56 percent of Americans 12 and older used tobacco, nicotine, alcohol or illicit drugs monthly, with 44 million having substance use disorder and 54.6 million adults (21 percent) having mental illness. SAMHSA.
