### DIGITAL HEALTH & AI

### REGULATORY & POLICY

**Medicare Reimbursement**
- CMS released proposed Medicare reimbursement rule establishing physician payment changes effective 2027: 1.68% conversion factor drop for non-alternative payment model physicians; 1.19% for alternative payment model participants; 2.5% Medicare pay reduction due to Congressional reimbursement increase expiration; MSSP Level E shared savings rate increase from 50% to 60%; MIPS transition to MVPs by 2029. Healthcare Dive.

**Public Health**
- Global Burden of Disease study published in JAMA Cardiology reveals ischemic heart disease deaths in United States fell 53% from 1990 to 2023, yet 88.8% of 2023 deaths (419,000 of 473,000) remained preventable; smoking-related deaths down 33.3%; particulate air pollution deaths down 74.9% since 1990; body mass index increased 12.5%; blood glucose levels up 10.5% since 1990. STAT News.

**Defense & Public Health**

Kennedy Jr. and Admiral Brian Christine support the initiative; FDA recently revised testosterone product labels removing warnings about prostate cancer risk. STAT News.

### CLINICAL RESEARCH & INNOVATION

**Neurotechnology**

- Stanford University research team demonstrated first-in-human clinical trial of "double neural bypass" hybrid neuroprosthetic system over 3 plus years combining intracortical brain-computer interface with targeted spinal cord and cortical stimulation to restore hand movement and sensation in 42-year-old male with complete C4 sensory/C5 motor tetraplegia; 61% increase in right elbow flexion force within 15 weeks of transcutaneous spinal cord stimulation alone; 86% increase in right elbow flexion force over 35 weeks (13.25N baseline to 24.65N); 62% increase in left elbow flexion force over 35 weeks (24.03N baseline to 38.90N); participant achieved functional abilities including self-feeding and hand-to-face movement. Nature.

**Genetic Research**

- Hebrew University researchers published findings in Nature describing a new synthetic gene circuit platform reducing genetic complexity in human cells for disease detection and response, with proof-of-concept demonstrated using IL-15 in human embryonic kidney cells. Healthcare IT News.
