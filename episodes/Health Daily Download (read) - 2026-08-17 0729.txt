## GLOBAL

**Life Sciences and Pharmaceutical**

- Massive Bio and Sequence Me Now partner to expand cancer clinical trial access; partnership links organizations' websites to increase oncology and hematology trial awareness and accessibility through AI-enabled patient-trial matching (Global)

- AST-120 demonstrated superior renal protection versus Lactobacillus probiotics in chronic kidney disease: retrospective TriNetX cohort of 680 matched patients showed AST-120 reduced stage 4 CKD progression risk by 70.6%, stage 5 CKD by 51.2%, ESKD by 66.7%, and all-cause mortality by 49.2% over 2.3-year follow-up (Science Progress)

- WHO reports 80% of new Ebola cases in eastern Democratic Republic of Congo originate from unknown transmission chains (STAT News)

**Digital Health Research and Implementation**

- Nursing leadership for digital NICU transformation systematic review of 18 studies (January 2013-January 2025) found nurse-led governance in NICU digital health implementation associated with 60% reduction in device-related pressure injuries, 30-day readmission rates of 13.8% (nurse-led telehealth) versus 30.9% (non-enrolled), and approximately 12-day shorter length of stay in developmental care trials; organizational/leadership support documented in 94% of studies, structured training in 44%, funding/IT infrastructure in 22%, clinical champions in 17% (SAGE Open Nursing)

- FDA and EMA approved 60 DHT-related drugs (39 EMA, 23 FDA) from September 2021 to March 2025, with ePRO tools dominating at 70% and smartphones at 66%, but no approvals based solely on DHT-derived primary endpoints (Therapeutic Innovation & Regulatory Science)

- Umbrella review of 161 systematic reviews identified 1,333 telemedicine barriers versus 504 facilitators (2.6:1 ratio); top barriers: high costs (n=106), technical issues (n=94), training gaps (n=91); zero systematic reviews from low-income countries (PLoS One)

- Systematic review of 16 studies identified AI/ML models predicting neonatal respiratory outcomes; only 2 studies included external validation; critical gaps in calibration reporting and post-discharge outcome prediction (Advances in Neonatal Care)

- Qualitative study of 14 individuals with eating disorder history or subclinical symptoms examined remote measurement technology risks; self-tracking supported recovery in those with greater distance from active illness but risked reinforcing compulsive patterns in those with residual symptoms (JMIR Form Res)
